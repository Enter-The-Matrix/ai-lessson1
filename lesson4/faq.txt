NORTHWIND LABS IT AND OFFICE FAQ (fictional, for practice)

Q: How do I connect to the office Wi-Fi?
A: Join the network called "Northwind-Staff" and sign in with your work email and password. Guests should use "Northwind-Guest"; the guest password is printed on the card at reception and changes every Monday.

Q: I forgot my password. What should I do?
A: Go to the self-service page at reset.northwind.example and follow the steps. You will need your phone for the security code. If you are locked out after 5 wrong tries, wait 30 minutes or call the IT help desk on extension 4400.

Q: When does a new employee get a laptop?
A: Every new employee receives a laptop on their first day. You can choose between a 14-inch MacBook Pro and a Dell XPS 15. Laptops are replaced every 3 years. If your laptop breaks, bring it to the IT desk on the 2nd floor and you will get a loan laptop the same day.

Q: Can I install my own software?
A: Yes, from the Company Portal app, which has over 200 approved programs. Anything not in the portal needs a request to IT, which usually takes 2 working days. Games and cryptocurrency mining software are never allowed on work devices.

Q: Is there parking at the office?
A: There are 40 parking spaces in the basement. Spaces are first come, first served, except 6 spaces reserved for electric cars with chargers and 4 spaces for people with disabilities. Bike storage with showers is next to the main entrance.

Q: What time is the office open?
A: The building is open from 07:00 to 21:00 on weekdays. At weekends you need your access badge and must sign in with security at the front desk. If you lose your badge, report it to security straight away; a new one costs 15 euros.

Q: Is lunch provided?
A: Free lunch is served in the canteen on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 12:00 to 14:00. On other days the canteen sells meals at a discount for staff. Fruit, tea and coffee are free every day on every floor.

Q: How do I book a meeting room?
A: Use the calendar app and add the room as a guest. Rooms can be booked for at most 2 hours at a time. If nobody checks in on the room's tablet within 10 minutes of the start time, the booking is cancelled automatically.
